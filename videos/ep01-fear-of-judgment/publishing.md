## Description

That tightening in your chest when someone looks at you — it isn't anxiety, it isn't weakness, and it definitely isn't something you should just get over.
It's one of the oldest signals in the human body, and it has a story worth knowing.

Understanding the fear of judgment changes how it feels. This video traces that feeling from its origins around prehistoric fires — where being cast out by your group was a death sentence — through ancient Rome, medieval town squares, and into modern psychology labs where brain scans revealed something remarkable: social exclusion and physical pain activate the exact same circuitry. The same word. The same alarm. The same *hurt*. By the end, you won't have a quick fix — but you'll have something more useful: a completely different way of seeing what that feeling actually is, and where it's really coming from.
