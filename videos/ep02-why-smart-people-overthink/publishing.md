## Description

There's a version of intelligence nobody warns you about — the kind that won't let you sleep.
It's the same mind that solves the hard problems, notices the subtle things, and then, at 1 AM, turns itself on a conversation from nine hours ago.

This episode explores why smart people overthink — not as a self-help fix, but as a genuine psychological mystery. From a 2,400-year-old Greek text asking why the sharpest minds were always the most unsettled, to modern brain scans showing intelligence and anxiety sharing the same neural signature, the evidence keeps pointing the same direction: overthinking and analytical thinking aren't opposites. They're the same machine. What you'll leave with isn't a technique or a hack — it's something quieter. A different way of looking at the mind you've been trying to slow down.
